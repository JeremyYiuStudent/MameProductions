The studio

Compact games, fast iteration, clear scope.

Mame — 豆, bean — is a name for something small. That is the whole idea: projects a handful of people can hold in their heads, plan honestly, and actually put in front of players.

Most of what we make starts in a game jam. The difference is what happens afterwards: we plan the scope with a small team, build the gameplay systems properly, coordinate a real release, and keep going once the jam deadline is gone. Visual novels, puzzle games, minigame collections, the odd horror maze — the genre moves around, the working method doesn't.

We currently have 3 projects under the studio banner, and members who ship engineering work on other teams' titles in between.
